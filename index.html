Tabletop Miniatures Games

Tabletop miniature games are a hobby that involves building and painting miniature figures.
These games are often expensive and time-consuming, but they can also be incredibly frustrating, since I am not very good at playing the game and my hands shake too much to paint well.
The most popular tabletop miniature game is probably Warhammer40k, but personally, I am looking forward to the StarCraft TMG.
